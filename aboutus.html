About JustAid

JustAid started with a simple yet powerful idea: “Legal help should be accessible to everyone, not just the privileged.”

Built by students, legal enthusiasts, and techies, JustAid is your one-stop legal guide — from AI-powered advice to real consultations.

“We believe justice should be simple, human, and within reach for all.”
